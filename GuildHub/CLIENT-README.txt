Vemal's Ears
============

Vemal's Ears shows your guild's consent-gated Discord voice captions in a
small movable window above World of Warcraft.

Vemal's Ears (By Downtime) is a separate application from Downtime's gear and
talent tools. Both may run together; each has its own guild connection,
settings and Start with Windows preference.

PAIR THIS COMPUTER

1. Open Vemal's Ears once. This lets Windows recognize its pairing links.
2. Open the one-use pairing link your guild administrator sent you.
3. Choose Open Vemal's Ears when the browser asks.

If the browser cannot open the link, choose Connect guild and paste the
complete one-use invitation. Confirm the guild name and server shown before
saving it. The app confirms your guild and starts the caption window.

The same generic companion is used by every guild. Your pairing link teaches
it which encrypted guild server to use. If the link expired or was already
used, ask your guild administrator for a new one.

LINK YOUR DISCORD ACCOUNT

If your guild requires voice-channel membership, choose Link Discord in the
Guild & Privacy card. Copy the command shown, open Discord, and run it in the
paired guild within five minutes. This links this computer to the Discord
account that runs the command; it does not change anyone's voice consent.

Join the exact voice channel where the caption session is active. Captions
stop and clear when you leave. Speech captured before you joined is not
delivered later, and captions from an absence are not replayed on rejoining.

MOVE THE CAPTIONS

Choose Move in Vemal's Ears. Drag the sample rows, then choose Save. The live
caption window is click-through and stays compact so it does not cover ground
effects.

Open the companion's gear menu to choose how long finished text stays visible
and whether one, two, three, or four speakers may be shown at once. Changes
apply immediately and affect only this computer.

MUTE A VOICE

In Caption display, choose Choose voices. Mute or unmute an individual speaker
there. Speakers appear after their first caption. This hides their captions
only on this computer; it does not mute Discord audio, stop their transcription,
change consent, or affect other viewers. Muting removes their existing rows.

PRIVACY

- Your computer receives caption text, never Discord voice audio.
- The guild's Discord bot token never comes to your computer.
- Your revocable device credential stays in Windows Credential Manager.
- Caption text stays in memory and is not written to an addon or log file.
- Each Discord speaker must consent before their own audio is processed.
- The local mute picker keeps only opaque speaker identifiers and display
  names, never audio or transcript text.

If this computer is lost or retired, ask the guild administrator to revoke it.
